Private by design

Owner contact details are not shown on public tag pages.

For item owners

Register your tag ID and item with the coordinator. Do not write phone numbers or home addresses on the tag.

For people who find an item

Enter the tag ID and the place where you found the item. The coordinator will help contact the owner.

Important for this prototype

This starter website saves demonstration data only in this browser on this computer. It is not yet a shared online system. For real use across different phones, connect a secure backend or a school-approved form and restrict coordinator access.
